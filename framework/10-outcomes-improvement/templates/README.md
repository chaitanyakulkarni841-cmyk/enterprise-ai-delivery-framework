# Blank outcomes templates

[Stage guide](../README.md)

Upload a benefits register, before/after scorecard, retrospective, improvement-backlog template, leadership recommendation, and retirement checklist.

Fields: metric, baseline, comparison period, assisted result, sample size, source, assumptions, projected/observed status, cost, limitation, owner, and next decision. Link to the adoption/outcomes workbook in stage 09 instead of duplicating it.
